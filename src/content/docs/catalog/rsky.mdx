An AT Protocol implementation in Rust by the Blacksky team, prioritizing community safety and self-governance. Apache-2.0 licensed — compatible with BuilderOS (AGPL family) and NOT on the vendor denylist (Meta/OpenAI/xAI are excluded; Blacksky is none of those).

**Maturity caveat, verbatim from their README:** "This library is a work in progress. Things will change. Things are incomplete. Things will break. Until the project reaches version 1.0.0, stability will not be guaranteed."

Component map (library crates on crates.io): `rsky-crypto`, `rsky-identity`, `rsky-lexicon`, `rsky-syntax`, `rsky-common`, `rsky-repo`. Services: `rsky-relay`, `rsky-pds` (Personal Data Server), `rsky-feedgen`, `rsky-firehose`, `rsky-jetstream-subscriber`, `rsky-labeler`, `rsky-satnav`, `rsky-wintermute`.

rsky ≠ rkyv (a serialization library). They are different projects; do not conflate them.
